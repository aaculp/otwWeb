Questions

Frequently asked.

How does OTW update venue signals?

OTW combines venue updates with community activity from guests who check in and contribute real-time information about wait times, crowd size, and energy levels.

Can I post recurring events?

Yes. Create a recurring event once (like Tuesday trivia or Thursday karaoke) and it automatically appears every week. Guests can save it and receive reminders before it starts.

Are flash offers free to send?

OTW offers free and paid plans. The free tier includes a limited number of flash offers per month. Available features may vary by plan as OTW grows.

What is an OTW Nudge?

A Nudge is when a nearby guest tells your venue they're interested in a deal. When enough people nudge your venue, you know there's real demand — and can send a flash offer at the perfect time.

Can breweries, coffee shops, and restaurants use OTW?

Absolutely. OTW works for any venue where people gather — bars, restaurants, breweries, coffee shops, wineries, food trucks, arcades, entertainment venues, and more.

Do guests have to download the app?

Guests can browse venues and events without creating an account. They need the app to save events, receive reminders, claim flash offers, and nudge venues.

Can I edit events after I post them?

Yes. You can update event details, change times, or pause recurring events at any time from the app.

Can multiple staff members manage our venue?

Currently one account manages each venue listing. Multi-user access for teams is on the roadmap.
