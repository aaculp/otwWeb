Questions

Frequently asked.

How does OTW know what's happening tonight?

OTW combines venue-posted events with community updates from guests who check in and share real-time information about crowds, wait times, and vibe.

Is OTW free to use?

Yes. You can browse all venues, events, and community activity without creating an account or paying anything.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse venues, activities, and see what's happening tonight without signing up. An account is needed to check in, save events, claim flash offers, and contribute updates.

Are events on OTW verified?

Events go through an approval process. Community members can also confirm or flag activities, helping keep information accurate.

Can anyone submit an event?

Yes. Any authenticated user can submit an event or activity. Submissions are reviewed before appearing publicly.

What cities does OTW cover?

OTW currently covers Columbus, Ohio with plans to expand to additional cities in the future.

How often is OTW updated?

Venue information is updated by businesses and community members throughout the day. Guest check-ins and activity reports help keep information current.
